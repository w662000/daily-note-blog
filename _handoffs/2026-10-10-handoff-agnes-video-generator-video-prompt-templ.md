
## Prompt Tips

1. **Be Specific About Visuals**
   - ✅ "身穿灰色道袍，双手掐诀" (wearing grey daoist robe, hands forming seal)
   - ❌ "一个修仙者" (a cultivator)

2. **Describe Lighting**
   - Add "电影级光影" (cinematic lighting), "金光闪闪" (golden light), "云雾缭绕" (misty)

3. **Specify Camera Movement**
   - "远景镜头缓缓推进" (long shot slowly zooming in)
   - "特写镜头" (close-up)
   - "广角镜头" (wide angle)

4. **Include Style Keywords**
   - "东方玄幻写实风格" (Eastern fantasy realistic style)
   - "4K画质" (4K quality)
   - "电影级" (cinematic level)

5. **Action Description**
   - Use specific action verbs: "施展" (cast), "环绕" (surround), "组成" (form)
   - Add motion: "剑光闪闪" (swords gleaming), "灵力波动" (spiritual energy fluctuation)

## Resolution Options

- **16:9** (横屏): width=1152, height=768 - YouTube, Bilibili
- **9:16** (竖屏): width=768, height=1152 - TikTok, Douyin, Reels
- **1:1** (方形): width=1024, height=1024 - Instagram

## Duration Guidelines

- 5-10 seconds: Best for single action shots
- 10-15 seconds: Good for scene establishment
- 15-20 seconds: Maximum for single clip, better to split into scenes
